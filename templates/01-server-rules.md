# 📜 PETABLOCKS Server Rules & Guidelines

Welcome to **PETABLOCKS**! To ensure a fair, friendly, and enjoyable environment for everyone, all players must adhere to the community guidelines outlined below.

---

## 1. General Conduct & Chat Rules
- **Respect All Players**: Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, racism, or toxicity of any kind will not be tolerated.
- **No Spamming or Advertising**: Do not flood chat, spam caps, or promote other servers/services.
- **Language**: Keep public chat family-friendly. Light banter is fine, but targeted insults or derogatory language will result in a mute.
- **English in Global Chat**: Please keep main chat in English so staff can effectively moderate. Direct messages (`/msg`) and party chats may use any language.

---

## 2. Gameplay & Fair Play
- **No Cheating or Hacked Clients**: The use of hacked clients, auto-clickers, X-Ray texture packs/mods, fly hacks, or unauthorized automation is strictly prohibited.
- **Bug & Exploit Abuse**: If you discover a duplication glitch or mod exploit, report it immediately to staff via Discord. Knowingly exploiting bugs will result in a permanent ban and inventory wipe.
- **AFK Machines**: Light AFK pools are permitted provided they do not generate excessive server lag or bypass auto-kick timers during peak player hours.

---

## 3. Griefing & Land Protection
- **No Griefing Claimed or Unclaimed Land**: Do not destroy, alter, or steal from other players' builds or storage without explicit permission.
- **Claim Your Land**: Always use the claim system (`/claim` or claim tool) to protect your base, farms, and storage.
- **No Trapping or Spawn Killing**: Setting up death traps, teleport traps, or killing players repeatedly in non-PvP zones is prohibited.

---

## 4. Account & Security
- **One Account Per Player**: Alternate accounts ("alts") used to bypass bans, farm rewards, or abuse claim limits are forbidden.
- **Account Responsibility**: You are responsible for all actions performed on your Minecraft account.

---

## 5. Punishment Escalation Policy

| Offense | 1st Warning | 2nd Offense | 3rd Offense |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chat Toxicity / Spam** | Verbal Warning | 1–24 Hour Mute | 7-Day Mute / Temp Ban |
| **Griefing / Stealing** | Rollback + 3-Day Ban | 14-Day Ban | Permanent Ban |
| **Hacked Clients / X-Ray** | 30-Day Ban | Permanent Ban | Appeal in Discord |
| **Duplication / Exploit Abuse** | Permanent Ban + Wipe | Appeal in Discord | Blacklist |

> **Need Help or Found a Rule Breaker?**  
> Open a ticket on our official Discord or use `/report <player> <reason>` in-game.
