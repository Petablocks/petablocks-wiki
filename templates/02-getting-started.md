## 📦 How to Install the Modpack

### Method 1: CurseForge App (Recommended)
1. Download and install the **CurseForge App** from [curseforge.com](https://www.curseforge.com).
2. Search for **PETABLOCKS** in the Minecraft section.
3. Click **Install**, then press **Play**.
4. Once in the multiplayer menu, connect to `play.petablocks.com`.

### Method 2: Prism Launcher / MultiMC
1. Open Prism Launcher → **Add Instance** → **CurseForge**.
2. Search for **PETABLOCKS** and select the latest release.
3. Allocate at least **6GB to 8GB of RAM** in instance settings for optimal performance.
4. Launch and connect!

## 🛡️ Protecting Your Base (Land Claims)

To protect your builds, chests, and machinery against griefing:
1. Hold a **Golden Shovel** (or type `/claim`).
2. **Right-click** one corner of the area you wish to protect.
3. Walk to the opposite corner and **right-click** again.
4. You will see gold blocks and glowstone outline your claim boundaries.

### Claim Management Commands:
- `/trust <player>` — Grants full build and chest access to a friend in your claim.
- `/accesstrust <player>` — Allows a player to open doors, use buttons, and levers.
- `/containertrust <player>` — Allows access to chests and machinery only.
- `/untrust <player>` — Revokes all permissions from a player.
- `/abandonclaim` — Removes the claim you are currently standing inside.

## 💰 Economy & Trading
- **Earn Money**: Sell resources at spawn, complete daily quests, or participate in player market stalls.
- **Player-to-Player Trading**: Type `/trade <player>` to open a secure direct trade GUI.
- **Check Balance**: Type `/balance` or `/bal`.
